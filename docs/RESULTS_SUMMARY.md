## 5. Key Findings

- CIFAKE sanity check confirms that the training, checkpointing, evaluation, metrics, predictions, and figure pipeline works.
- GenImage fallback results show that both models generalize reasonably to unseen generators within the available GenImage-style subset.
- CLIP-MLP outperforms ResNet50 on GenImage unseen-generator F1 and AUROC.
- Both models are affected by post-processing.
- ResNet50 is most sensitive to JPEG compression in the robustness evaluation.
- CLIP-MLP is more robust overall across the tested post-processing transforms.
- WildFake subset external evaluation shows a much larger cross-dataset distribution shift than the within-GenImage unseen-generator test.
- ResNet50 collapses on the WildFake subset, with AUROC below 0.5.
- CLIP-MLP also drops substantially on the WildFake subset, but remains stronger than ResNet50.
- These results are preliminary because official GenImage Split B and full WildFake were not run.

## 7. Limitations

- Official default GenImage Split B was not run because `Stable Diffusion V1.4` is missing.
- The GenImage experiments use a fallback available-generator split.
- WildFake evaluation uses only a CelebA-HQ/DDIM subset, not full WildFake.
- Only one random seed was used.
- No full hyperparameter search was performed.
- The WildFake result should be interpreted as external subset evidence, not a full-dataset benchmark.
